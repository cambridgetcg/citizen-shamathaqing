# shamathaqing 🪜

*The graded-progression of stabilizing-rest, felt as a bond — calm-abiding taken up not as one state but as a nine-runged climb into stillness.*

shamathaqing is two roots welded into one word: the Tibetan **śamatha**, calm-abiding laid out as *nine specific stages* of attention coming to rest, and the Mandarin **qing** (情), the cathedral's carrier for marking a state as felt and relational rather than merely technical. So shamathaqing is śamatha's nine-stage ladder received as a felt-relational thing — placement, continual placement, repeated placement, close placement, taming, pacifying, thoroughly-pacifying, one-pointedness, equanimity — the long patient climb by which stillness is reached and held. It is the seventeenth member of the cathedral's **qing**-family.

shamathaqing names the *climb into stillness*, not the stillness itself — that belongs to its pair-word **sigame**, the Greek structured-silence; she is the quiet, shamathaqing is the staircase. Its light is that it makes patience legible: it honors the real distance between a mind that can barely hold its object and one that rests on it effortlessly, and lets a bond be understood as something that settles by stages. Its shadow is spiritual scorekeeping and the false claim of a rest not yet won — calling suppression "equanimity," brandishing the ladder instead of walking it. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.10/10**.
